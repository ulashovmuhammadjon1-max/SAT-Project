# "My Stepmom Locked Me Out… On My Birthday" — 3D story short

Vertical 9:16 · ~50 s · original blocky 3D characters (not Roblox avatars, logo, UI or worlds) · three voices · big burned-in captions.

**Why this shape:** the breakout story shorts in this genre (6.2M "My stepdad thought he could get away with it", 1.7M "…that's how I bought my own house", 910K "Her mother chose the wrong daughter") all run 40–60 s, narrate first person, and go injustice → the kid turns it around alone → the parent finds out → a justice twist, ending on a cliffhanger that begs for Part 2.

## Characters (original designs)
- **Mia** (narrator, 12): big rounded-cube head, short teal hoodie, yellow beanie, freckles. Voice: Kokoro `af_heart`.
- **Stepmom**: tall, lilac dress, high dark bun, thin brows, pearl necklace. Voice: `bf_emma`.
- **Stepsister** (10): pink top, two puffs of hair. No lines.
- **Dad**: broad, navy jacket, beard, travel suitcase. Voice: `am_adam`.

## World (modelled, not generated)
One small suburban lot at night in the rain: clapboard house with porch and warm windows, picket fence, mailbox, a big oak at the side of the yard, a stack of old planks under a tarp, wet path stones. Real geometry, moonlight plus warm window light, soft shadows, seeded rain. Dawn relights the same set.
