## Facts used
- Equatorial rotation speed ≈ 1,670 km/h (40,075 km ÷ ~23.93 h); the poles barely move.
- If the solid Earth stopped, the atmosphere (and oceans) would keep their momentum.
- With no rotation, one solar day lasts a year: about six months of light, then six months of dark at the pole.
- Without the centrifugal bulge, oceans migrate toward the poles and a continuous land band appears around the equator (Esri analysis by Witold Fraczek, 2010).
The spin speed on screen is sped up for visibility.
